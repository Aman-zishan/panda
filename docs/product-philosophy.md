# Product philosophy

Approved 2026-11-17. PANDA earns its place on the desk when physical presence makes
an action faster, calmer or easier to understand.

## Device and app

| On the device | In the companion app |
| --- | --- |
| Capture a quick spoken thought and confirm it with sound, light, posture or a glance. Use text briefly, such as a reminder time. | Read and edit full transcripts. Browse reminders, notes, to-dos and calendar details. |
| Start or pause focus, snooze or acknowledge with one knob action. Show the current state in the tiny world. | Handle multi-step choices, history, search, accounts and settings. |
| With presence hardware, notice arrivals and departures. Give a timely nudge, then return to quiet. | Set up, troubleshoot and configure the device. |

Keep the device silent during meetings and respect physical microphone mute.
Exclude scrolling dashboards, feeds, chat screens, keyboards, touch interfaces and
long spoken replies. Calendar, notes and task management stay on the phone or desktop.

Stage 1 preserves growth when focus ends early. With presence sensing, deferring a
break nudge may briefly droop a plant. It recovers within one day, even without action. See the [tiny-world rules](tiny-world.md).

## Feature review

1. Does a phone already handle this screen task well?
2. Does desk presence make the interaction faster, calmer or more meaningful?
3. Can someone understand the result in one glance, one sound or one knob action?
4. Does it reduce distraction?
5. Would it still feel calm several times a day?

A device feature must pass questions 2–4. Move detailed interactions into the app
or drop the feature.
