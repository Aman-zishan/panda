Overview

PANDA stands for Presence-Aware Non-verbal Desk Assistant. This documentation describes the planned device behaviour and build requirements. Hardware validation is still pending.

Product rules

  • Do not duplicate a task a phone already handles without distraction.
  • Use sound, light, posture and the knob for immediate feedback and control.
  • Keep transcripts, lists, history and settings in the companion app.
  • No feeds, scrolling dashboards or human-language speech output.
  • The tiny world must recover without punishment or permanent loss.

Full product philosophy ↗

Basic and Connect

Basic: a self-contained desk companion with physical controls, sounds, a focus timer and a tiny world.

Connect: adds voice understanding, reminders, notes, calendar integration and a companion app for details and settings.

Both experiences are planned. Build files and availability will be documented as they are released.

Build stages

  1. Breadboard: microphone, speaker, OLED, encoder and ESP32-S3. Validate capture, confirmation and focus sessions over three weeks.
  2. Presence and privacy: add radar, electrical microphone mute and meeting behaviour.
  3. Enclosure: measure the parts, test the collar and head movement, then fit the electronics into the body.
